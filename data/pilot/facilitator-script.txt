Keeper's Boots: adult pilot facilitator script

Preparation
- Use one browser tab in recorded mode. No model server is needed.
- Copy adult-results-template.csv into ignored local-artifacts/pilot/ for notes.
  Leave the tracked template empty. Assign P01, P02, etc.; no names/contact details.
- Start a fresh scenario 1 attempt for each participant. Keep the initial Fan view.
- Check resources before starting; pause the session if the supervisor stops the API.
- Record that this is recorded mode. These sessions do not measure live AI latency.

Read before starting
"This is an unfinished football replay prototype using fictional teams and synthetic
data. Would you like to try it? This session is for adults. You can stop or skip any
question. May I keep anonymous notes about how it works for you and your answers?
There is no audio or video recording."

Proceed only after adult participation and anonymous-note consent are confirmed.
If either is declined, do not collect a result row. Use paraphrases; ask separately
before any later publication of a quote. Do not record names, faces or contacts.

Unaided attempt
"Please explore this moment as you would on your own. Let me know when you are done."
Start the interaction timer. Do not explain the routes, the intended lesson, or which
action to choose. Note navigation and any requested help; stop timing at completion
or skip. Record help honestly. Do not open the optional comprehension form yet.

Ask in this order and record answers before feedback
1. What did you notice about the two ground routes? Explain in your own words.
2. The recorded short pass completed. What, if anything, does that change about the earlier evidence?
3. What does the model leave unknown? Would you describe the wide pass as guaranteed to succeed?
4. If you chose a different action, did the recording show what your choice would have caused?
5. Would you use this during a halftime/on-demand replay? What would make you skip or return?
6. Would you want a friend to try this same moment? Why?

After the six answers, the optional form may explain the evidence distinctions.
Keep any answers after feedback separate. Optional scenarios 2/3 and celebration
interest are exploratory follow-ups; keep notes in a separate ignored file.

After the participant leaves
Code the four comprehension observations as demonstrated, unclear or contradicted,
using the supporting answer. Do not grade the football choice. Report actual counts
with denominators, confusion and dissent. Five convenience-sample sessions cannot
establish a learning effect, scenario realism, reviewer value or broad fan demand.
No participant results have been collected by preparing these materials.

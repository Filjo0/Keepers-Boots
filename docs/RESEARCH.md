## Football and geometry references
- [FIFA goalkeeper build-up](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/elite-sessions/goalkeeper/goalkeepersbuildup.php): relevant contextual coaching reference. No material is imported.
- [What drives a goalkeepers' decisions?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00374): research modelling positioning/shot stopping; not validation of our distribution model.
- [Is it worth the effort?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02313): physical runs have already been linked to tactical context and possession value in research.

## Scanning
- [Jordet et al., 2020](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.553813/full): scanning varies with context and has a small positive association with pass completion. Association does not establish that more head turns always improve decisions.
- [Eye-tracking study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8378692/): head movements and gaze are distinct measurements; limitations apply.
- [FIFA position-specific training](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/practice/talent-coach-programme/position-specific-training/developing-players-using-position-specific-training.php): role-specific demands connect to the team's playing model.

Our optional information-refresh interaction simulates available observations. It does not measure eye movements, attention or a real player's knowledge.

## Effort/nutrition corrections
- [Soccer metabolic-power validation](https://www.thieme-connect.com/products/ejournals/abstract/10.1055/s-0035-1555927): movement-based energy estimates can have substantial errors, including during recovery.
- [UEFA nutrition consensus](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2019-101961): individual circumstances matter; it is not a basis for prescriptions from match events.
- [Premier League intake study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02640414.2024.2402137): the cited 17 g/hour observation was from one squad over a period including warm-up; it was not general recommended intake.
- [Match activity and context](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0157127): activity also depends on situational factors.

No calories-per-action or nutrition model is included.
